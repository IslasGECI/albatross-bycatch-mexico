- HOURGLASS — Opening wide (the global pelagic MPA gap); Resolution local (a case study of Mexico's albatross colonies). The global opening is repaid qualitatively in Discussion move 3.
- OPENING — Seabirds are among the most threatened groups of birds, and the tool for protecting them at sea is anchored to land, so pelagic species are missed.
- CHALLENGE — No study has quantified how much Laysan albatross core area in Mexico falls inside MPAs; we ask where it lies and test the hypothesis of limited coverage.

## INTRODUCTION

- I1 Hook — seabirds among the most threatened groups of birds, and the causes of their decline.
- I2 Pelagic gap — MPAs are a conservation tool, but they sit on the coast, so pelagic species receive less protection.
- I3 Mexican case — the Laysan albatross nests on Guadalupe, Clarion, and San Benedicto; the islands and their waters sit in protected areas; fishing works those waters.
- I4 Why tracking — the areas must be located before coverage can be tested, and tracking locates them.
- I5 Concepts — fix the core-area term and distinguish MPA from the broader protected-area term; full definitions stay in Methods.
- I6 The gap — no study has quantified how much of the Laysan albatross core area in the Mexican Pacific falls inside existing MPAs.
- I7 Novelty — the first pooled core-area analysis for Mexico's albatross colonies.
- I8 Question + hypothesis + approach — where the core area lies and how much the MPAs cover; the hypothesis of limited coverage; the mapped overlap from pooled tracking.

- M4 Overview — why track2KBA, the workflow in six steps without function names, the promise of detail, and the archived analysis code.
- M5 Preparation — the records consolidated; the tracks split into trips; incomplete trips and trips that stayed within 60 km removed; the survivors are the foraging trips.
- M6 KDE — the ARS scale as the KDE smoothing parameter; the 50% UDs per individual.
- M7 Trust — the representativeness assessment: whether the sample's areas hold for the population; the resampled subset; the locations left out; the threshold it sets.
- M8 Define — the foraging component as the cells whose 50% UDs reach that threshold; the core area as its union with the 60 km disk.
- M9 Place and Measure — the overlap with the existing MPAs; the overlap index, its formula, and the meaning of 1 and 0.
- M10 Ethical considerations — permits, minimization, no harm.

## DISCUSSION

- D1 Answer — the questions answered: the percentage and the spatial answer, the core area offshore while coverage hugs islands.
- D2 Limitation — the data cover the breeding season only; the conclusions apply there, which is also the most relevant period.
- D3 Circle — the aggregate hides placement: protection is anchored to islands while the core area sits beyond every boundary; the global coverage study is cited qualitatively.
- D4 Contribution — the paper evaluates existing MPAs rather than proposing new areas; island-anchored protection leaves the pelagic core area uncovered; the foraging-component layer is available for planning.
