# Discussion

Mexican MPAs do not protect the core area within the EEZ used by Laysan albatross nesting.
Only {{overlap_index_percentage}} percent of the core area falls inside MPAs.

The main limitation is that the data cover only the breeding season.
Therefore, the conclusions apply only to that period.
However, this period is the most relevant for protection, given that the population could be most vulnerable during the breeding season.

In Mexico, MPAs surround islands, while the albatross foraging component of the core area is offshore, unprotected.
This coverage deficit agrees with what has been reported by @critchley2018marine for pelagic species.

In Mexico, the core area of the Laysan albatross extends beyond the boundaries of existing MPAs, which are primarily island-anchored.
Currently, establishing and enforcing an MPA in offshore waters is hard; therefore, alternative options should be explored.
One option is to incorporate data from other species using the same core area.
This broader perspective would help determine which management strategies are appropriate for the core area.

In its unprotected core area, Laysan albatross faces potential threats.
Commercial fisheries bycatch is the most significant at-sea threat to the species [@copello2014spatial]; assessing bycatch risk in this area is a next step.
