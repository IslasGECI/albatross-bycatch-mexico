
# Results

\FloatBarrier

Fig 1 shows the routes of the Laysan albatrosses.
We see that individuals from both Guadalupe Island and Clarion Island use the same marine areas.
In particular, the trajectories of individuals from both colonies pass along the coast of Baja California.
The trajectories of individuals from San Benedicto Island approach the coasts of Baja California Sur.

The asymptote in the inclusion curve in Fig 2 indicates that the sample size is adequate.
The sample of 121 individuals has a representativeness of XXX%, which means that it adequately represents the Laysan albatross population at the three colonies.
We can state that the results obtained from this sample are applicable to the population.

![

Gray points show bootstrap estimates of the inclusion rate for each sample size, and the black line is their mean.
The gray band is the confidence interval, and the 93.4% value is the representativeness achieved with all 121 tracked individuals.

Fig 4 shows the 50% UD delineated as purple polygons from the continuous N_IND surface.
This area is the one most individuals share as 50% UD.

The methodology for delineating the 50% UD requires removing the data within a radius around each colony.
Following [@hernandez2019sexual], a radius of 60 km around each colony was used.
We define the core area as the union of the 50% UD and the 60 km rings around each colony.

The 50% UD measures XXX km2, and the 60 km rings around the colonies measure XXX km2.
In total, the core area spans XXX km2, of which XXX km2 are within a Mexican marine protected area.
That is, XXX% of the core area is protected.
